# Oridam

Every Google account comes with 15GB of storage.

When it fills up, most people do the same thing.
Create another Google account.
Another 15GB.
Problem solved.

Except...

Now your memories are scattered.
Your documents are scattered.
Your storage is fragmented.

Oridam lets you use all your Google accounts as one.

No more switching accounts.
No more remembering where things are.
Just one place.

**The complexity belongs to the software, not the user.**
