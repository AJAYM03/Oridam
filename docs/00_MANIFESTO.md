# Oridam Manifesto

> **Multiple Google accounts. One place.**

Every Google account comes with 15GB of storage.
When it fills up—usually because of Google Photos—most people do the same thing:
Create another Google account.

The problem isn't getting more storage.
The problem is the fragmentation that comes after it.

Every new account means:
*Which account has space?*
*Where are my college documents?*
*Are those photos in my personal account or my backup account?*

Oridam exists to remove that friction.
Not by replacing Google.
Not by becoming another cloud provider.
But by letting you use all your Google accounts as one.

Users shouldn't think about accounts.
They shouldn't think about quotas.
They shouldn't think about storage allocation.

They should simply upload a file.
Search for a document.
Browse their memories.
And trust that everything is exactly where it should be.

The complexity belongs to the software. Not the user.

If someone opens Oridam and forgets that their files are actually spread across five different Google accounts...

Then Oridam has done its job.
